HOW TO READ THIS MODEL

From a cue to a changed response.

Projection neurons encode two odor cues. Sparse Kenyon-cell activity reaches two mushroom-body output pathways: approach and avoidance. Delayed dopamine signals modify their synapses. The same cue can then produce a different readout.

What this demonstrates

Separate learning memories, inspectable synaptic updates, reversal, frozen controls, and persistence across restarts.

What still needs experimental evidence

A learned cue association does not establish improved maze navigation, multi-step planning, or biological fidelity. Several assays primarily test reflexes. Compare matched seeds, frozen controls, and held-out trials before claiming a behavioral learning effect.
