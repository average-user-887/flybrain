What this result does and doesn’t show
This is immediate binary choice using downstream spike counts from the full fixed connectome. Directly stimulated retinal and lamina cell classes are excluded from the readout. Reward updates only the external linear readout; the model has no delayed memory, simulated walking, or synaptic learning inside the connectome. Trial order, stimulation dropout and intensity vary by seed. Frozen-control and shuffled-label results test the readout association; they do not show that biological wiring is necessary.
